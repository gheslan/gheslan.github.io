Legal notice and privacy

Who publishes this site, and what happens to your data when you visit it.

Publisher

This is the personal, non-commercial website of Guewen Heslan, PhD candidate in economics. Contact: guewen.heslan@u-pariscite.fr.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages — GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, United States (github.com).

No cookies, no tracking

The site sets no cookies and measures no audience. The site loads nothing from third-party servers: fonts, scripts and images are all served from this site. There are no advertising, social-media or tracking scripts.

Language preference

The language of the site is chosen in your browser from its language setting and time zone; nothing is sent anywhere. If you use the FR/EN button, your choice is stored in your browser’s local storage (key lang) so that it is kept on your next visit. It never leaves your device and you can delete it at any time from your browser settings.

Hosting logs

Like any web host, GitHub may record technical data such as visitors’ IP addresses to run and secure its service. I have no access to these data. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Email

If you write to me, your address and message are used only to reply to you and are not passed on to anyone.

External links

Links to other sites (ORCID, LinkedIn, GitHub, universities…) are subject to those sites’ own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to access, correct and erase data about you, and to object to their processing: write to guewen.heslan@u-pariscite.fr. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL.
