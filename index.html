Guewen Heslan.

  • PhD candidate in Economics, LEMNA — Nantes Université
  • Temporary Teaching and Research Assistant (ATER), Université Paris Cité

I study how environmental policy reshapes trade flows and logistics networks — the economic mechanisms that translate regulation into the real-world movement of goods and capital.

My doctoral research, at LEMNA (Nantes Université) in collaboration with LHEEA (Centrale Nantes), combines structural gravity models of trade with fleet and freight optimization models to study how carbon pricing and environmental regulation affect maritime trade and the decarbonization strategies of shipping companies. I am supervised by Rodica Loisel, Corinne Bagoulla and Pierre Marty.

Interests: international trade, environmental and energy economics, maritime transport, carbon pricing.
